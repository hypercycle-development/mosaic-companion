## 1. What PALM Economy Is

**PALM Economy** is a shared economy powered by the $PALM token where tokenized
Real-World Assets (RWAs) drive growth, inclusion, and lasting value.

**Palmyra Ecosystem** (by zenGate) is the product layer — the software that
makes traceability, compliance, market access, and payments work on the ground.

| Entity | Role |

| **PALM Economy** | Token layer: $PALM staking, rebates, burns, DAO governance |

| **Daniel Friedman** | Founder & CEO |
| **Theodore Morisis** | Co-Founder & Director of Business Development |

### Core Narrative
> "A complete, blockchain-powered ecosystem that brings end-to-end traceability,
> compliance automation, and direct market access under one roof."

### The Four Value Pillars
1. **Traceability** — Farm-to-fork tracking with zero blind spots
2. **Compliance** — EUDR-ready, due diligence automation
3. **Market Access** — Direct buyer connections, bypass extractive middlemen
4. **Payments** — Blockchain-based settlements

### The 6-Step Traceability Checklist
1. Farm Registration & Geo-Mapping
2. Digital Identity for each producer
3. Production Data Capture (harvest, inputs, labor)
4. Batch-Level Traceability (farm → aggregation → transport → export)
5. Due Diligence Statement (risk assessment, EU system upload)
6. Record-Keeping & Audit Readiness (5+ years, inspection-ready)

## 4. Commodities: What We Sell Traceability For

### Cacao (Primary — Start Here)
- **Major LATAM origins:** Ecuador (Arriba Nacional), Colombia, Peru
- **Grades:** CCN-51, Nacional, Trinitario, Criollo
- **Harvest calendars:** Main harvest Oct–Jan (Ecuador); mid-crop May–Jul
- **Price drivers:** ICCO daily price, organic premium (20–40%), single-origin premium
- **EU relevance:** EUDR applies to cacao (deforestation risk commodity)
- **Key buyers:** Lindt, Barry Callebaut, Valrhona, craft chocolate makers

### Honey (Secondary)
- **Major LATAM origins:** Argentina, Uruguay, Brazil, Mexico
- **Types:** Polyfloral, monofloral (eucalyptus, citrus, wildflower)
- **Certifications:** Organic, EU organic, fair-trade
- **EU import:** Significant EU honey demand; traceability = price premium
- **Key buyers:** Retail brands, pharmaceutical (medical-grade), boutique food

### Palm Oil (Phase 2)
- **Major LATAM origins:** Colombia, Ecuador
- **Uses:** Food, cosmetics, biodiesel
- **EU relevance:** EUDR HIGH priority commodity; deforestation regulation strict
- **Traceability gap:** Most palm oil chains are opaque — huge opportunity

### Coffee (Future)
- **Already some traceability** but blockchain upgrade = next level
- **Opportunity:** Premium single-origin + blockchain story = 50%+ price premiums

## 8. EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR) — The Urgency Driver

### What Agents Must Know
- **Effective:** December 30, 2024 (large companies); June 30, 2025 (SMEs)
- **Applies to:** Cattle, cocoa, coffee, oil palm, rubber, soy, wood
- **Requirement:** Companies placing these on EU market must prove products are
  deforestation-free and comply with local laws
- **Due diligence:** Operators must collect geolocation data, risk-assess, and
  submit declarations to EU system
- **Penalty:** Products can be blocked at EU ports; fines up to 4% of EU turnover

### Sales Angle
> "EUDR isn't optional — it's the law. The question isn't whether to trace, it's
> whether you'll trace with paper (expensive, error-prone) or blockchain
> (automatic, auditable, ready for inspectors)."
